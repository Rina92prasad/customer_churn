# customer_churn
This analysis explores key drivers behind customer churn by examining service usage patterns and their correlation with customer retention. The dataset was visualized using various count plots and segmented insights, leading to the following conclusions:

1 Internet Service Impact

* Customers with Fiber optic internet show a significantly higher churn rate (~42%) compared to those with DSL (~15%) or no internet (~5%).

* This may indicate dissatisfaction with service quality or pricing for Fiber users.

2 Online Services & Support

* A strong trend emerged where customers not subscribed to services like:

    OnlineSecurity

    OnlineBackup

    DeviceProtection

    TechSupport

* These customers showed a churn rate exceeding 40–50%, while those using these services had churn rates under 20%.

* This suggests that value-added services play a major role in customer retention.

3 Streaming Services & Multiple Lines

* Churn among customers who use StreamingTV and StreamingMovies is slightly lower (~25%) than those who don’t use them.

* Customers with MultipleLines show a balanced churn rate (~27%), suggesting it’s not a major churn driver.

4 Phone Services

* Churn is relatively even between those with and without PhoneService, though customers without phone service exhibit slightly higher churn (~30%).
